title: "The weekly rhythm"
---
The week is the unit the app is built around. Two short lessons: how a week is planned, and how it is reviewed.

## 2.2 Three priorities [#01-three-priorities]

At the start of a week (Monday, in the app's time zone) you choose **three to five objectives**: results you want to have by Friday, not a task list. Each one is a line of text; you can carry an unfinished one over from the previous week, and you can attach time entries from Work to it.

The Today page shows the open objectives all week. On Friday and at the weekend the week block starts pointing at next week's plan, so the ritual does not slip.

## 2.3 The review [#02-the-review]

The review takes about fifteen minutes and answers six questions, written as free text under a summary the app prepares for you: lessons marked done per day, inbox entries archived, hours on objectives against other hours, achievements logged, opportunities that gained evidence and ones that have gone stale.

The six questions:

1. What was the biggest win this week?
2. What pulled time away from the priorities?
3. What did I learn?
4. Am I satisfied with this week?
5. What changed my mind this week?
6. Which opportunity gained evidence?

Answer briefly. The value is in reading last month's answers, not in writing long ones.

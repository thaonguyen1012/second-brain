# 2.2 Three priorities

At the start of a week (Monday, in the app's time zone) you choose **three to five objectives**: results you want to have by Friday, not a task list. Each one is a line of text; you can carry an unfinished one over from the previous week, and you can attach time entries from Work to it.

The Today page shows the open objectives all week. On Friday and at the weekend the week block starts pointing at next week's plan, so the ritual does not slip.

# 2.3 The review

The review takes about fifteen minutes and answers six questions, written as free text under a summary the app prepares for you: lessons marked done per day, inbox entries archived, hours on objectives against other hours, achievements logged, opportunities that gained evidence and ones that have gone stale.

The six questions:

1. What was the biggest win this week?
2. What pulled time away from the priorities?
3. What did I learn?
4. Am I satisfied with this week?
5. What changed my mind this week?
6. Which opportunity gained evidence?

Answer briefly. The value is in reading last month's answers, not in writing long ones.

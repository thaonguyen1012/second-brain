# Module 2. Around the handbooks

Studying is one of six areas of the app. The others exist so that what you read turns into something: a note, a decision, a week that went somewhere. This module walks through them in the order you are likely to meet them.

## The six areas

| Area | Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | `/` | where you left off, this week's priorities, the inbox count, the morning brief |
| Week | `/tuan/` | three to five objectives, then a 15-minute review |
| Growth | `/phat-trien/` | handbooks, roadmap, skills, the job market, achievements |
| Library | `/thu-vien/` | items (courses, books, articles, projects) and notes |
| Opportunities | `/co-hoi/` | business hypotheses with evidence for and against |
| Work | `/cong-viec/` | client projects and a timer |

## How the areas connect

An article lands in the inbox, becomes an item in the library, gets a note when you are done with it, and may end up as evidence for an opportunity or a skill. The week review asks whether any of that moved. Nothing is required: each area works on its own.

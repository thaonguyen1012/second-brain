# 2.1 Items and notes

An **item** is anything you are working through: a course, a book, an article, a film, a project. It has a type, a status (planned, active, done, dropped) and a source link. A handbook can be an item too; the Library offers to add every imported handbook as a course in one click.

A **note** is markdown attached to one or more items. The editor supports headings, lists, task boxes, tables and `[[` links to other records. Notes are saved as you type, and your data export (the "Download a copy" link on the Handbooks page) writes them back out as `.md` files with front matter.

The rule the app nudges you towards: an item that is **done** should have an output. When you mark a course finished, its card asks for a note, and offers a template with a place for highlights and three key ideas. You can ignore it; the reminder only goes away once a note exists.

Notes never leave your server. The background AI jobs tag inbox links and news from their title and first lines; they are never sent a note.
